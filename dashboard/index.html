The Urbanization Paradox Explained

While total urban population is strongly correlated with electricity consumption (r = +0.931), the urbanization percentage exhibits a slight negative correlation (r = -0.147). This is an empirical artifact of geographic scale: small Union Territories like Chandigarh (>97% urbanized) and Delhi have dense urban proportions but compact total consumption relative to agricultural megastates like Uttar Pradesh and Bihar.
